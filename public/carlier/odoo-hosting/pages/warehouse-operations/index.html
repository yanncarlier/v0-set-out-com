Questions

The honest answers.

What's included, what it costs, and where the boundaries are.

What is included in Odoo ERP Hosting?

A managed Odoo 18 Community instance with Nginx, PostgreSQL, and Odoo workers pre-configured, automated SSL certificates, root-level security hardening, 24/7 uptime monitoring, and unlimited users and records. Automated off-site backups are an optional add-on.

Is this Odoo Enterprise or Odoo Community?

Odoo ERP Hosting runs the open-source Odoo Community edition. It is not an Odoo Enterprise licence and does not include Enterprise-only apps. If you need those apps, use Odoo's Enterprise plan directly.

How many users can I add?

Unlimited. There are no per-user, per-module, or per-database fees on the plan. Add the whole team without watching a user counter.

How much does it cost?

€55 per month, or €594 per year. The annual plan works out to €49.50 per month — a saving of €66 compared with paying monthly. Prices are in EUR and billed through Stripe. Optional off-site backups are €240.

Can I cancel my plan?

The monthly plan can be cancelled anytime and stays active until the end of the current billing period. For annual billing questions, contact hello@carlier.site.

Are backups included?

Automated off-site backups are an optional add-on with a custom retention policy. The base hosting plan does not include automated off-site backups, so add the backups plan if you need them.

Can I migrate my existing Odoo database?

Migration is not included by default. Contact hello@carlier.site with your current Odoo version and database size to discuss whether a migration can be arranged.

Do I get root access to the server?

No. Odoo ERP Hosting is managed hosting. You get the Odoo application; server administration, Nginx, PostgreSQL, SSL, and monitoring are handled for you.

Who runs Odoo ERP Hosting?

Odoo ERP Hosting is part of Carlier, an independent software company that builds and operates web and AI products globally. Odoo is a trademark of Odoo S.A.; this service is not affiliated with Odoo S.A.
