Questions

The honest answers.

What Lousi does, what it costs, and where a human teacher is still the better choice.

Is Lousi really free to try?

Yes. The free trial includes voice tutoring, real-time corrections, and camera vocabulary — the whole loop, not a locked demo. No credit card to start. Subscribe only if you keep learning.

What does Premium cost?

$9.99 per month or $44.99 per year — about $3.75 per month. Premium adds unlimited tutoring sessions, session recaps, and the full correction history. Billed through Google Play.

Does it teach Mandarin too?

Lousi is built specifically for Cantonese — voice practice, corrections, and vocabulary for spoken Cantonese. For Mandarin, Carlier has a separate app: Learn Chinese.

How is this different from a flashcard app?

Flashcards drill words in isolation. Lousi holds a real spoken conversation, corrects you while you talk, and can name the objects you point your camera at.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. AI Agent Lousi is Android-only. Email hello@carlier.site if you want to be notified about iOS.

What are session logs and corrections?

Every session can be revisited: the app keeps voice logs of your practice and the corrections it gave, so you can review what you practiced and what got fixed. The full history comes with Premium.

Is this exam prep or a certification?

No. Lousi is a practice tutor — the tutoring is informational, not a certified language exam or an academic grade.
