Questions

The honest answers.

What Coin Identifier does, what it costs, and where a professional grader is still the better choice.

Is the value in the app what I can sell the coin for?

No. It's an estimated range for information only — not a dealer offer, NGC/PCGS grade, or what eBay will pay. Condition, mint marks, and errors can move the real price a lot.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Coin Identifier is Android-only. Email hello@carlier.site if you want to be notified about iOS.

US coins only, or world coins too?

World coins. The example on this page is a Hong Kong 2 Dollar. Accuracy still varies by lighting, wear, and how unusual the piece is.

How do the 3 free scans work?

Download on Google Play and scan up to three coins with identification and a value estimate. Premium is $4.99 per week or $9.99 per month for unlimited scans and full collection tools. No credit card is required to try the free scans.

Will it read worn, dirty, or tiny coins?

Often, if you can get a sharp photo. Use zoom and focus in the scan screen. Heavily worn or error coins may need a specialist after the app's first pass.

Do I need a Google API key?

Identification uses Google Gemini. In Settings you can add a free key from Google AI Studio. The key stays on your device. Google's quotas and terms apply.

What data do you collect?

Scan photos go to Google Gemini for identification. Your collection stays on the device. Details are in the privacy policy.
