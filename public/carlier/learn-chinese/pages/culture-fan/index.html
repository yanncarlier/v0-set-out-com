  • Keep talking instead of switching to English

Questions

The honest answers.

What Laoshi does, what it costs, and where a human teacher is still the better choice.

What is AI Agent Laoshi?

AI Agent Laoshi is an Android app for Mandarin conversation practice. It uses live voice conversation, camera-based vocabulary, real-time corrections, and translation support to help you speak Mandarin in context.

Do I need to know any Mandarin to start?

No. You can start with the Mandarin you already have, including absolute beginner phrases. Laoshi works from what you can say and builds from there.

How does the free trial work?

The free trial gives you access to voice tutoring, corrections, and camera vocabulary before you subscribe. The official Laoshi page says no card is required to start.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is $9.99 per month or $44.99 per year, billed through Google Play. The annual plan works out to about $3.75 per month — a saving of about 62% compared with paying monthly.

How does camera vocabulary work?

During a session, switch to the front or back camera and describe what you see. Laoshi helps you name objects, learn their usage, and put them into sentences.

Is Laoshi a replacement for a human teacher?

Laoshi is built for conversation practice and real-time feedback. For exam preparation, accent coaching, or advanced literary study, a human teacher is still the better choice.

Can I use Laoshi for HSK or exam prep?

Laoshi is a conversation practice tool, not a certified exam course. It doesn't issue grades or certificates. Use it alongside a structured exam course if HSK preparation is your goal.

What device do I need?

An Android phone or tablet with Google Play and a microphone. A camera is recommended for camera vocabulary practice.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play. Open Google Play, go to Subscriptions, select Agent Laoshi, and cancel. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Is my data private?

Google Play's Data Safety section for Agent Laoshi lists no data collected and no data shared with third parties. You can read the full privacy policy for details.
