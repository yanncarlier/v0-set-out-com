Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how Carlier (“we”) handles information when you use the Coin Identifier Android app and this landing page. Contact: hello@carlier.site.

What we collect

Coin photos you scan are processed so the app can identify the coin and return an estimated value range. Collection entries you save (images, names, notes, estimates) stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself.

If you add a Google Gemini API key in Settings, that key is stored locally on your device so identification can run. We do not operate a cloud account for your collection.

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you send (usually an email address and the message).

How identification works

Scan images are sent to Google’s Generative AI / Gemini service to produce a name, year, country, rarity context, and a market-value estimate. That processing is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not sell your photos or collection data.

This website

Google Play download links on this page include campaign parameters (source, medium, campaign, button name) so we can see which landing-page button led to a store visit. We do not run a third-party analytics pixel on this page today.

Your choices

Delete saved coins in the app, clear app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data. You can remove a stored API key in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the applicable age).

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date on this page.
